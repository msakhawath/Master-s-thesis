## What is this study about?

This study evaluates a conversational robot that conducts simulated job interviews. You will
take part in a short practice interview with the robot and then answer a questionnaire about
how the interaction felt.

**The robot is being evaluated — you are not.** There are no right or wrong answers, your
performance is not assessed, and nothing you say is judged.

## What will I be asked to do?

1. Take part in a simulated job interview with the robot, lasting about 10 minutes.
2. Complete a questionnaire about your experience, taking about 10 minutes.
3. Answer three short open questions.

Total time: approximately 40 minutes.

## Do I have to take part?

No. Participation is entirely voluntary. You may:
- decline to take part;
- skip any question;
- stop the interview at any moment, without giving a reason;
- withdraw your data afterwards (see below).

There is no disadvantage of any kind if you decline or stop.

## What is recorded?

- **Your speech during the interview** is transcribed into text. Transcription is performed by
  **AssemblyAI**, a cloud service outside the EU. Audio is streamed to that service for
  transcription and is **not** stored as an audio recording by the researcher.
- **The text of the conversation**, together with timing measurements (for example, how long
  the robot took to respond).
- **Your questionnaire answers** and your written answers to the open questions.
- Depending on which version of the system you use, the conversation text may additionally be
  sent to **OpenAI**, a cloud service outside the EU, in order to generate the robot's replies.

Please **avoid mentioning names, addresses, employers, or other identifying details** during
the interview. If you do, they will be removed from the transcript.

## How is my privacy protected?

- You are identified only by a code such as `P07`. Your name never appears on the
  questionnaire, the transcript, or any research file.
- The list linking your name to your code is stored separately, on an encrypted drive, and is
  destroyed as soon as data collection ends.
- This signed form is stored separately from all research data.
- Only the researcher and, if required, the supervisor have access to the data.
- Results are reported in aggregate. Any quotation used in the thesis is anonymised, and
  anything that could identify you is removed.

## How long is my data kept?

Research data is kept until the thesis has been examined and any resulting publication is
complete, and no longer than 2 years, to be confirmed with BHT, after which it is
deleted. Signed consent forms are retained separately as required by BHT.

## Can I change my mind?

Yes. You may withdraw your data at any point up to two weeks after your
session by contacting the researcher and quoting your participant code. Your data will
then be deleted. After that point, data may already have been aggregated into the analysis
and can no longer be separated out.

## Risks and benefits

There are no anticipated risks beyond those of an everyday conversation. Some participants
find speaking to a robot mildly unfamiliar at first. There is no payment for participation.
Your contribution helps evaluate whether small, self-hosted language models can support
useful human-robot interaction.
