## Definitions — read before each session

**Premature interruption (PI).** The robot's speech begins while the participant is still
producing an utterance. A brief overlap on the participant's final word counts. Backchannels
from the participant ("mm", "yeah") while the robot speaks do **not** count.

**Awkward delay (AD).** After the participant has clearly finished and stopped speaking,
more than about three seconds pass before the robot begins. Count once per occurrence,
not per second. Use a watch or count silently — do not estimate afterwards.

**User repetition (UR).** The participant restates the same content because the system
seemed not to register it. Rephrasing for their own reasons does not count.

**Failed turn (FT).** The robot's response is empty, garbled, unrelated to the question
under discussion, or does not follow from what the participant said.

**Repeated question (RQ).** The robot asks a question substantively already asked earlier
in the same session.

> Two of these — PI and AD — are the behavioural counterparts of questionnaire items Q5
> and Q4. Comparing what you observed with what the participant reported is worth a
> paragraph in the Results chapter, whichever way it comes out.

**Consistency warning:** you are the sole observer, so your counts are subjective and
cannot be checked for inter-rater reliability. Say so plainly in the Limitations section,
and apply these definitions identically from P01 to P20.
