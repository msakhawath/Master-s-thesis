INTERPRETATION
--------------
The VAP+TurnGPT condition reduced endpoint decision delay and end-to-end FTO in every
complete matched pair, while multi-agent graph latency did not show a reliable condition
difference. The final predictive trials all yielded through the TurnGPT-timeout path rather
than the direct VAP-yield path. VAP therefore functioned primarily as a speaking/hold gate
in this dataset; the observed timing reduction must not be attributed to VAP alone.

Because several early repetitions used the same short response rather than the scheduled
distinct phrase, stimulus diversity was lower than planned. However, the two conditions
remained closely matched within every retained pair, and the protocol-adherent n=9
sensitivity analysis shows the same conclusion.
